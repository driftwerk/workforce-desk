# Licence FAQ

Desk is **source-available** under the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](LICENSE).
This page explains, in plain terms, what that allows. It is a guide, not part
of the licence: if anything here differs from [LICENSE](LICENSE), the licence
wins.

## The short version

You may use, copy, change and share Desk for free, for any purpose — at home or
at work, including inside a company. You may **not** use Desk to offer a
product or service that competes with it, such as selling Desk (changed or
not) or running it for others as a paid service.

## Can I…

| | |
|---|---|
| Use Desk for my own work, or at my company | **Yes** |
| Change it for myself or my team | **Yes** |
| Share copies, changed or not, with the licence and notices | **Yes** |
| Build a plugin, theme or integration for Desk | **Yes**, as long as it isn't a competing product |
| Sell Desk, or a changed copy of it | **No** |
| Offer Desk to others as a hosted or paid service | **No** |
| Remove or obscure the licence or the copyright notice | **No** |

Desk ships with the Workforce hub, which has its own licence (the Elastic
License 2.0; see [NOTICE](NOTICE)).
