# Desk for Chrome: privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Desk for Chrome ("the extension") lets the Desk app on your computer use your Chrome for you. This policy says what the extension handles and where it goes.

## What the extension handles

When Desk's agents work in your Chrome, the extension handles, in the tabs it opened only:
- the addresses and titles of those pages;
- their text, or their HTML;
- screenshots of Desk's own window;
- what Desk types or fills in on those pages.

It never reads, changes or takes pictures of your other tabs or windows. It doesn't read your history, bookmarks, cookies or saved passwords.

## Where it goes

Only to the Desk app on the same computer, through Chrome's native messaging. The extension makes no network requests of its own, and its developers run no server that receives anything from it.

What the Desk app then does with a page, such as showing it to the AI model you chose for an agent, is set by you in Desk. That model provider's own policy applies to what Desk sends it.

## What is kept

The extension keeps two things:
- which tabs and window are Desk's, until Chrome closes;
- a key that identifies this Chrome profile to Desk, made on your computer and never sent anywhere. Desk receives only its public half.

The Desk app keeps an activity list of what its agents did in your Chrome, with screenshots, for up to 90 days. You can see it in Settings → Browser, and erasing a person in Desk erases theirs at once.

## Sharing

None of this is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
